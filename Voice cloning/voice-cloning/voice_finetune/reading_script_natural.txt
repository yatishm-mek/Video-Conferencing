# Natural speech script: about 30 minutes of speech (about 45 minutes to record).
# Each person reads the same script. Lines starting with # are notes and are not read.
#
# How to record (this matters more than anything else):
#  - Don't read it like a script. Look at the line, take it in, then SAY it the way you'd
#    say it to a friend. Your own rhythm, your own ups and downs.
#  - Say each line in one flowing go, with NO pauses in the middle.
#  - Then stop for about one full second before the next line.
#  - Made a mistake? Pause, and say the whole line again. Bad pieces are dropped.
#  - Quiet room, same microphone and same distance every session (about a hand's width).
#  - No music, fan, or other voices. Don't let the recording get too loud.
#  - One session = one file (about 4 to 5 minutes). Take a break and drink water in between.
#  - Keep every person's recordings in their own separate folder.
